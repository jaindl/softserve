Shape & surface

Radius

Cards 20px. Inner rows 12–14px. Buttons and tags full pill (200px). No square corners anywhere — that was v1's signature, and it's retired here.

Borders

1px at 12–14% opacity of the text color. No glow, no grain, no chalk underline. Depth comes from a single soft shadow on screenshots only.

Screenshots

White card, 14px radius, 2° tilt, one shadow. Hotplate does this with phones; you do it with Claude / Airtable / LinkedIn captures.

Posters (4:5)

Three starting templates, named by their ground: the Black, the Cream, the Mint. Say the name; the template key is in the caption. Each uses one accent; the rest is ground + text. Footer stays "Personal Brand, AI, & LinkedIn Growth" in italic display.

Rules

Do

  • Sentence case headlines. Caps only in 13px labels.
  • One pastel per poster; pick it by meaning (mint = win, peach = old way, pink = hero).
  • Headline ≤ 9 words, light weight, tight tracking.
  • Real screenshots, tilted 2°, on a white card.
  • Marker font for a single hand note, max one per slide.

Don't

  • Bold uppercase Garamond titles (v1).
  • Chalk underlines, grain texture, glow-fill cards.
  • Pastel outlines on dark — fill them instead.
  • Square corners or the 13px cream frame.
  • More than two type sizes per poster beyond footer.
